# Why I Built PnPDaily

*By Martin Gonzalvez | March 13, 2026*

---

One thing that has been bugging me for a while now is how spread out the print and play community is. We are not exactly tiny, but we do seem to live in separate neighborhoods that do not always talk to each other very much. You have people on Facebook in **Martin's PnP Hideaway**, people on Reddit in **r/printandplay**, and people on BoardGameGeek posting in the monthly **"What print and play games are you crafting or planning on crafting this month?"** and **"PnP Games on Your Table"** geeklists. All of those are active, useful places. All of them have good people in them. But most of the time they still feel like separate silos.

That is not meant as a knock on any of those communities. I like all three of them. I am in all three of them. I get useful ideas, inspiration, tips, leads, and good conversation from all three of them. But because I move around between those different corners of the hobby, one thing has become obvious to me: there does not seem to be a lot of cross-pollination between them.

That is where the idea for **PnPDaily** came from.

I wanted a site that could pull together bite-size bits of useful, interesting, current PnP content from these different centers of gravity and put them in one place. Not a giant portal. Not some bloated "everything for everyone" site that tries to swallow the whole hobby in one gulp. Just a clean page with small widgets that update dynamically and give you a quick sense of what is going on in the wider PnP world beyond your usual hangout.

At heart, that is what this site is supposed to be: a compact daily check-in for people who love print and play games, whether they spend most of their time on Facebook, Reddit, BGG, or some combination of the three.

Right now the site is still young, but even in this early form it is already doing a few things I wanted.

The **Tip of the Day** widget pulls in useful tips from members of the Hideaway Facebook group. That one matters to me because the Hideaway is full of practical PnP knowledge, and a lot of that knowledge tends to disappear into the scroll. Somebody drops a genuinely useful crafting or printing tip, people react to it for a day or two, and then it gets buried under the next wave of posts. Pulling some of that into a rotating tip widget seemed like a good way to keep those ideas visible.

The **PnP Crowdfunding Roundup** widget highlights some print and play projects that are on crowdfunding right now. That is another area where it is easy for people to miss things. If you are not plugged into the right feed, group, or thread at the right moment, a perfectly interesting project can slide right past you.

The **PnP Community Poll** widget gives me a place to share results from polls we run in the Hideaway from time to time. I like polls because they are a quick way to take the temperature of the community. They are not holy writ, obviously. But they are useful little snapshots of what PnPers prefer, argue about, or keep coming back to.

The **Current BGG Contests** widget is there for a very practical reason: if you are not already a regular BGG user, it can be weirdly difficult to find out what print and play contests are currently running over there. That information exists, but it is not always obvious to outsiders where to look. I wanted one place where non-BGGers could glance over and immediately see what is active.

The **Work-in-Progress Thread** widget might be the one I am most excited about, because there are so many good-looking PnP designs in development on BGG that never seem to get much visibility outside that ecosystem. There are designers over there quietly building really interesting stuff, posting playable files, getting feedback, iterating, improving. If you never browse WIP threads, you may not realize how much cool, playable material is sitting there right now.

And that gets to the larger point.

I do not think the PnP community is actually as fragmented in spirit as it looks from the outside. I think we are fragmented in location. That is not the same thing. People in these different silos are often interested in the same things: clever formats, useful crafting advice, hidden gems, work-in-progress designs, contests, crowdfunding projects, how to print better, how to store better, how to make the hobby easier and more fun. The problem is not that we have nothing in common. The problem is that we are often having parallel conversations in different places.

My hope is that **PnPDaily** can become a site that PnPers from any one of those silos can check on a daily basis, get a small glimpse of what fellow PnPers elsewhere are talking about, and feel a little more connected to the wider hobby. Maybe a Reddit regular discovers a BGG contest they would never have heard about otherwise. Maybe a BGG forum regular sees a useful crafting tip that started in the Hideaway. Maybe someone from Facebook notices a work-in-progress thread and jumps in with playtest feedback that helps a designer tighten their game.

That would be a win.

I also have plans to expand the site over time. I want to add more widgets that surface the latest topics, questions, and conversations happening across these different PnP centers. The goal is not to replace those communities. That would be silly, and honestly impossible. The goal is to create a small, useful bridge between them.

So that is why I built this site.

If it works, it should help the hobby feel a little smaller in the good way. Not smaller as in diminished. Smaller as in more connected, more legible, easier to keep up with, and easier to bump into people and ideas you might otherwise miss.

And as always, I am very open to feedback and suggestions from you all. If there is something you would like to see on **PnPDaily** that would make it more useful, more relevant, or just plain better, I want to hear it. This site is for the PnP community. It should be shaped by what that community actually needs, not just by whatever I happen to think looks neat on a page.
