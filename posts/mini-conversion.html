Daily PnP Roundup: Miniature Conversions

By Alex Chen | March 7, 2026

When I first encountered print and play gaming, I imagined paper miniatures only. Cardstock tokens, flat character sheets, maybe some die-cut pawns. But the community has evolved dramatically since those early days of PDF downloads and home printing.

Today's PnP gamers are converting in ways that would have been unimaginable five years ago. We're moving beyond simple token-based representation toward immersive 3D gaming experiences that rival tabletop hobbyists' elaborate collections.

The New Frontier of Miniature Crafting

The term "conversion" typically refers to the act of modifying existing miniatures or components to create something new. But in the print and play world, this encompasses a much broader spectrum of techniques. From resin casting using silicone molds from 3D printed masters to laser-cut layers creating dimensional figures, PnP gamers are leveraging maker tools at an unprecedented scale.

Popular Conversion Techniques

  1. Hybrid Approaches: Combining PnP templates with resin-cast or 3D printed miniatures
  2. Layered Paper Sculptures: Stacking cardstock layers to create 3D figures and terrain pieces
  3. Die-Cut Assembly: Complex paper engineering techniques for articulated pawns
  4. Photo-Realistic Tokens: Using inkjet printers on specialty printable vinyl substrates

Materials Matter

The choice of materials determines both durability and aesthetic quality. Laser-cut birch plywood has emerged as the gold standard for dimensional miniatures, offering structural integrity while maintaining that classic tabletop gaming feel. Meanwhile, polymer clay resins enable fine detail reproduction from scaled-down designs.

For larger runs, some makers are turning to vacuum forming techniques adapted for home workshops. This allows single molds to produce dozens of identical game components with acceptable cosmetic quality for play surface visibility.

Community Impact

What makes this particularly exciting is the open-source nature of these creations. The PnP community thrives on sharing: CAD files, mold designs, printing tips. A maker in Tokyo might develop a conversion technique perfect for board games, which spreads to Berlin within days through forums and social media.

This cross-pollination means innovation accelerates exponentially. One person's experimentation with an unusual material becomes the week's trending hack. The collaborative ethos transforms individual discoveries into shared knowledge that elevates all makers.

Getting Started

If you're new to PnP conversions:

  • Start small with paper-based dimensional pieces before tackling resin or wood
  • Invest in quality adhesive systems—good bonding is crucial for durability
  • Experiment with texturing techniques like sanding, priming, and painting
  • Join the conversation on reddit's r/printandplay to see what others are creating

The possibilities extend far beyond basic tokens. Every gaming table can become a showcase of creativity where imagination meets technical skill. The PnP community proves that great games don't need massive budgets or factory manufacturing—they need passionate makers willing to experiment.
