It's that time of the year again! Come design with us, for kids and families! And welcome to the

Children & Family Game Design Contest Logo


This is the 11th edition of the contest!  🥳

🏆 Check out the results!

The contest was originally started in 2015 as Children's Game Print & Play Game Design Contest by Chris Hansen. See more about the contest's history.

This contest is meant to design games that we can play with the children, or games that children can play together. The focus should be on games that children (9 and under) can play with their families. Themes should be appealing, mechanisms should be easy to grasp. As has always been the case, these games should include some player agency. Meaning games where you flip a card or roll a die and move to the next space of the same color are out, unless accompanied with something that requires at least a tiny bit more thought.

📄 Contest Form
All entrants are required to create an account on the Design Contests Hub website and fill in the Contest Form. You're not in the contest until you do!

📅 Contest Dates
Contest starts: January 15th, 2026
Game submission deadline: April 15th, 2026
Edits/tweaks allowed until May 1st, 2026
Voting begins: May 1st, 2026
Voting closes: May 15th, 2026

📚 TLDR: Changes this year

Same as last year, your game can compete in only one main category.

Technical changes

Contest Rules

Game Design & Components

E The only design restriction for this contest is that game be appropriate for the intended player age range. Game mechanics, theme, and other considerations open to the designer's discretion. Designers may work in teams, as designers, artists, playtesters, etc.

E There are no limits on quantity of components or rulebook size. Designers should be aware that larger and more complex games may be played by fewer people, and receive correspondingly less feedback. In addition, complex games can be more challenging for children to play and enjoy.

E All games must be printable on regular A4/American Letter Paper. Large format components such as game boards are okay as long as they can assembled from pieces printed on A4/Letter sheets. Here is a useful document for determining the margin size of sheets designed to be printed on either A4 or Letter paper.

E Designers may design any components necessary for their game, but the preferred format for files is PDF.

E Designers may ask players to provide a reasonable number of common game components, including but not limited to pawns, dice, cubes, standard playing cards, etc.

Game Submission

1️⃣ Create a WIP (Work In Progress) thread for your game in the WIP Forum.

The first post of this WIP thread must contain the following:

• A link to the 2026 Children & Family Game Design Contest thread (this very thread).
• A brief Game Summary (2 or 3 sentences).
• An image of the game (more than one is fine, but at least one is required). The image may be set up to replicate the game in play.
• Description of components required to play.
• Number of players.
• Intended earliest age to play.
• Approximate playing time.
• Category in which the game will be competing - either Children Game or Family Game.
• Links to the current game files. Rules and components should be in PDF format. The files can be hosted on your own webserver, online storage sites (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) or in your personal BGG image gallery. The files must be freely available to anyone with the link and users should be able to download them without needing to create an account.

All of the above information will be required in the WIP thread. Any other info is at the designer's discretion. Please keep the first post in your WIP thread updated with current links and information about the game.

E Include the current status of your game in the WIP thread's title. This will help other people know where you feel your game is in the design process.

Idea Phase (Private Playtesting. No publicly available printed components yet.)
Components Available (Public Playtesting. Printed Components are available. Game may be changed as a result of playtesting.)
Contest Ready (Game is complete. No changes will be made.)

E Games submitted to this contest should be new or contain significant updates to a previously released game, be it PnP, published, or part of a previous contest. Previously released games may not be entered unchanged.

2️⃣ Fill the Contest Form.

E To enter a game in the contest, you'll need to create an account on the Design Contests website. Please allow up to a day to receive the confirmation geekmail (it is sent by hand).

E After you activate your account and log on the site, enter the data about your game in the Contest Form. Your game is not in the contest untill you do this!

3️⃣ Post about your game in the contest thread.

Don't forget this step!

Submission Limits

Designers or design teams may submit up to 2 games to this contest.
The contest is meant for games that are still being developed, not for games that have already been published, are in the process of publishing or running a crowdfounding campaign.

Feedback requirement

Entrants are required to post meaningful feedback on at least two other games for each one that they enter. This requirement will be validated.
If you give feedback out of the game's thread (i.e. by geekmail, on comments on google drive, etc), please send me a note, so it's counted.

Prizes and Categories of Competition
Two Main Categories

1 Best Children's Game - This is a game intended for young children and doesn't require reading, maths or understanding complex rules.
2 Best Family Game - This is a game of moderate complexity, intended to be fun for both children and adults. Suggested minimum player age for this category is 8+.

Your game can compete in only one main category. These categories will receive 200 for 1st place, 150 for 2nd place, and 100 for 3rd place. Please tip this thread if you wish to support this contest.

Additional Categories

3 Best Rulebook - All games are automatically entered in this category.
4 Best Theme - All games are automatically entered in this category.
5 Best Art - Opt in category. To be eligible for this category, the game must have original art (created specifically for the game, and not AI art), and the artist must be credited by name or alias.

Voting

The contest includes a two-week period after the submission deadline to play the games and vote. No changes to the rules or components will be allowed during this time.

Voting will be done on the Design Contests website, and this part will be developed with the help of Ben Morayta.

To vote, you'll need to create an account. Please allow up to a day to receive a confirmation geekmail (it is sent by hand). If you already created an account when entering the contest, you're all set.

Copyright

The rights to all games submitted to this contest remain with their designers. You may release your game under any license you wish provided that the files are available for free, legal printing and assembly for personal use during the contest duration.

Designers must have full legal right over the games entered into this contest. Games should not contain artwork or characters that are protected by copyright or trademark without permission from the owner. Games may include content protected by Creative Commons or Copyleft licenses so long as the rules regarding redistribution and derivative works are followed. Artwork or characters in the public domain are allowed. AI art and stock art (if used according to its license) is allowed. Please declare your art sources.

Digital Versions

Digital adaptations via Tabletop Simulator, Vassal, etc. are allowed in this contest, provided printable PnP components are also available.

Happy Designing!

The most important part of this contest is to have fun and help each other create and develop fun, new games for the kids! Engaging with each other to build a community encourages better design and creates a better experience for us all. Thank you all in advance for being kind, courteous, and helpful members of this PnP community.

Happy designing!

The contest rules are adapted from those created by Chris Hansen for the previous Children's Game Print and Play Design Contests. The contest logo is adapted from the old logo by Chris Hansen.

IMPORTANT DATES

ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

Please check these requirements carefully to ensure that your entry is valid.

⭐ Games entered in this contest should be purpose-designed wargames. Games may be solitaire, two player, multi-player or even co-operative. However it is critical that the game is a wargame. The primary focus should therefore be on conflict and warfare. For the avoidance of doubt, the following themes are allowed: historical war from any time period (e.g. the Irish civil war), hypothetical war from any time period (e.g. hypothetical invasion of Cornwall by Korea in 1248), fantasy war (e.g. ents versus fungusfolk), sci-fi war (e.g. Cthulhu versus Godzilla on Planet X). Any scale of conflict is allowed from 1 on 1 (e.g. gladiators) through to grand strategic (e.g. whole of WW2). If you are in doubt about whether your idea qualifies, please consult BGG's very detailed definition of what a wargame is.

⭐ All games entered in the contest should be new. To be eligible for this contest, a game must meet the following criteria:
➡️ The game cannot have been entered in a previous contest unless it was not completed (i.e. withdrawn or uncompleted entries).
➡️ The game can be entered into other current contests, provided it is allowed in that contest's rules AND in entering, you can manage the time & effort to participate in this contest by developing your game and providing quality feedback to other entries.

➡️ The game cannot be publicly offered for download on BGG, a WIP thread, or other websites prior to the earliest date specified above. The game may be described and discussed online so long as it is not actually publicaly available for download.

➡️ You may develop a previously existing game into a new game for the contest. The metric for this is simple: If you already owned the previous one, would it be worth it to purchase the new game? That's the answer to the question of if the game is new.

⭐ You may not publish or launch a crowdfunding campaign for your entry during the contest timeframe. Once the contest is done, you may take steps to publish your game entry but please do not do so until voting in the contest has completed. If your game is ready for crowdfunding so soon, there's a good chance it's either not new or not well developed enough.

⭐ Designers need to have full legal rights over the games entered into this contest. Games should not contain artwork, fonts or characters that are protected by copyright or trademark without permission from the owner. Games may include content protected by Creative Commons or Copyleft licenses so long as the rules regarding redistribution and derivative works are followed. Artwork or characters in the public domain are of course allowed.

⭐ All game files should be freely available online for the duration of the contest. Game files may be hosted on your own web server or online storage sites such as Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, etc. The files must be freely available to anyone with the link and users should be able to download them without needing to create an account on the hosting service.

⭐ Each designer may submit up to two games. Collaborative designs are welcomed. Collaborators are encouraged to independently provide feedback on other entries.

⭐ For the benefit of all, entrants are encouraged to play and post feedback on at least three other games for each one that they enter.

➡️ If you enter a game, you must give meaningful feedback on at least three others. If you enter two games, give feedback on six others. Not only does this benefit both designers, it helps to make the voting results meaningful. Feedback can be in the form of commenting on any aspect of the game in the game's thread, or in direct message to the designer. Even if you are unable to fully test a game, giving the rules a read and pointing out something you like, dislike, find compelling, or confusing, is very helpful to the designer.

⭐ The organizer reserves the right to make the final decision on any issues arising from the rules.



SUBMITTING AN ENTRY

To submit your game to the contest, complete each of the following steps.

1️⃣ Create a "work in progress" (WIP) thread in the Works in Progress forum. If you mistakenly post your WIP in a different forum, such as the Design Contest forum or the Board Game Design forum, this will not disqualify your submission. However, most people looking to browse the entries will head to the WIP forum, so you will receive more traffic and feedback if you post to the proper forum.

2️⃣ In the top post of your WIP thread, include the following information in this template:

Entered into the 2026 Wargame Contest (make this a link) ( "[Xthread=3627732][/thread]" (remove the X)

Game Title: Fortress Assault
Players: 1-2
Time: 60-90 minutes
Rules: A publicly accessible link to your rules
Credits: (Not needed if you did everything yourself, but feel free to put your name here if you want. If you collaborated with anyone else, including graphics or if you want to give a shoutout to your lead playtester/rules editor, put that here in Credits)
