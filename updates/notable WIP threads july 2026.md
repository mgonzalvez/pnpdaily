Behind The Curtain
Establish and build your spy network behind enemy lines at the height of the Cold War.
A solo game of worker placement and card tableau/engine building played over five stages,
with an emergent narrative of espionage, tension, and daring driven by an AI event deck.
Can you complete enough clandestine missions to uncover valuable state secrets,
or will the secret police uncover and crack down on your fledgling spy network?


Game and Graphic Design: Martin Gonzalvez
Main Artwork: ChatGPT, Z-Image Turbo, Flux2
Icons: The Noun Factory
Game Mechanisms: worker placement, tableau/engine building
Vibe and inspirations: My biggest inspiration was a 2014 iOS game called Spymaster that allowed you to set up a spy network across Europe and complete various espionage missions. In 2024 I tried and ultimately failed to design a noir game called Veil of Shadows.. Last year, I made a rretheme of LOTR Adventure Deck Game with a noir espionage art style that I really liked. I also made a Cold War retheme of the game Cursed?!. In 2023 I designed Do Not Go Gentle, which was my first attempt at a thematic worker placement game. I have always loved tableau building / engine building games like 51st State: Master Set. So I swirled all of these inspirations, ideas and previous projects around in my head for several months, and out popped this: a thematic worker placement game where you play a neophyte spymaster tasked with setting up a spy network in a hostile rival nation. You start with one safe house, two agents, and a small number of low-level action spaces. As you play you will have the opportunity to expand your spy network, enhancing your ability to complete more advanced missions against higher-value targets. As you progress, your network expands, but it comes at a cost: the secret police become increasingly more aware of your network, and start to track you down in an effort to arrest your agents and roll up your spy network.
My hopes: Right now I've got a rough turn sequence, some ideas for Network cards, the Mission structure, the enemy AI, and how the game will scale up in challenge from early to mid to late game. I hope I can deliver on my vision of a thematic worker placement game, lean heavily into the Cold War, noir, and espionage elements, and successfully create a game that I feel doesn't exist yet in a form quite like this, and that I personally will enjoy playing. If some of you out there enjoy playing this game as well, so much the better. 🙂

Dice Delve is a solo Dungeon Crawler using just 9 Dice and 2 cards!

Dice Delve Image

Gameplay: during the game the player will be moving through a 3x3 map of dice. The number on each dice represents the encounter that you will face in that room, with some being good, and some being bad. But be warned! After you encounter a room, you will re-roll it, meaning that the map is constantely changing.

Aim of the Game: reach the exit with 15 Gold, without dying along the way!

Currently the game only includes 1 map, the Cavern, but more are on the way!

Captain Crash

1p | 20 min | 12+


This is an entry in the 2026 Solitaire Print and Play Contest

"That ought to do it. The Flarbothrusther should hold out just long enough to get us safely back home Tibbs.... Tibbs??? Oh no, he's run off again! Blast! And that Forget-Me-Fog isn't helping one bit, I can barely remember anything about him!"

Melvin is a kid with a vivid imagination and a stuffed cat named Tibbs as his best friend. Today their adventure has gone wrong. Crashed on an alien planet, lost in the Forget-Me-Fog, and Tibbs has wandered off! Worse still, the planet is home to Zark, a monster that can clone himself as anyone he wants. Now Tibbs is out there, but so are Zark's copies, and the fog is making it almost impossible to tell them apart. It's up to Melvin to find the real Tibbs and escape before it's too late. Good luck Melvin!

Captain Crash! is a solitaire deduction game. To find Tibbs, you must solve two independent puzzles. Only by solving both of them can you determine which Clone is really Tibbs and escape together. But be quick, if Mom calls for dinner, you're too late!

Note: any references to other kids and their stuffed animals are intentional and meant as a tribute to the joy of youth, the power of imagination and the sheer silliness of life
