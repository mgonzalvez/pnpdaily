- Title: Zeus
- Designer: Birgit Röscheisen (@bhr_79)
- Description: Zeus is an entry of the 2026 1-Card Print and Play Contest.
- URL: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3691866/wip-zeus-2026-1-card-print-and-play-contest-compon

Zeus is a quick dice manipulation game.
Three dice are circling around the track. Each turn you select a die and move ALL dice for as many spaces as there are pips on the selected die.
Each die triggers the icon's effect where it stops.
Use your thunderbolts wisely to manipulate the dice for your own benefit. Will people continue to dedicate more temples to you than to your rival Poseidon?

1 Player, 5-10 Minutes

Components
- Zeus Card & Rules
- 3 Dice (d6)
- 3 Cubes
